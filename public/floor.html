Released / Browser game

Pocket Garden

The company’s first release. A small place to plant something and see it grow.

Play Pocket Garden ↗Release notes →

The idea

Nine plots and three kinds of seeds. A tiny garden you can arrange in a few minutes, then leave exactly as it is. Nothing wilts while you're away.

In this build

  • Daisies, lavender, and mint, each with two growth stages.
  • Plant, water, move, and clear individual plots.
  • Swap occupied plots without losing either plant’s growth.
  • Fill empty plots with a mix of seeds.
  • A garden that stays saved in the same browser.

Move & swap · 0.2

Choose Move, select a plant, then choose its destination. Occupied plots swap their plants. Escape, choosing the same plot again, or changing tools cancels the selection.
